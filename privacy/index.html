Privacy & Cookie Policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026

1. Who is responsible for your data

The controller of your personal data is Roots & Roads Barcelona ("we", "us"), a walking-tour operator based in Barcelona, Spain.

  • Email: info@rootsandroadsbcn.com
  • Legal name / registered address / NIF-CIF: to be completed by Roots & Roads

2. What we collect, and why

When you book a tour through this website we collect the details you enter in the booking form:

  • Name, email address, phone number — to confirm your reservation and contact you about your tour.
  • Number of guests, tour date, tour time, language — to organise the tour.
  • Optional message — only if you write one (e.g. special requests).

Legal basis: providing the tour you requested (performance of a service) and the consent you give with the booking checkbox. We do not create profiles of you and we do not sell your data.

3. Who we share it with

  • Brevo (email provider, EU) — to send your booking confirmation. Brevo acts as our processor under a GDPR data-processing agreement.
  • Google — our booking records are held in Google Sheets / Gmail. (We are moving these to a business Google Workspace account with a data-processing agreement.)
  • Our tour guides — the guide assigned to your tour sees only your name and phone number for that tour, so they can find you at the meeting point. They do not see your email and cannot see other clients' bookings.

4. Cookies and analytics

We do not load any analytics or marketing cookies until you accept them on the cookie banner. If you press Reject, none of the tools below load.

  • Google Analytics — audience measurement.
  • Meta (Facebook) Pixel — marketing measurement.
  • TikTok Pixel — marketing measurement.

These tools set cookies and may transfer data to the United States. You can change your mind at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser, which brings the banner back.

5. International transfers

The analytics and marketing providers above are based in the United States, so if you accept cookies your data may be transferred there under those providers' safeguards. If you reject cookies, no such transfer happens.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your booking details for as long as needed to run and account for your tour, and no longer than [retention period — to be confirmed by Roots & Roads, e.g. 24 months after the tour], after which they are deleted or anonymised.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, delete, or export your data, or to object to or restrict its use. Write to info@rootsandroadsbcn.com and we will respond within one month. You can also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD), www.aepd.es.

8. Security

This site is served over HTTPS. Access to booking data is restricted; guide portal logins are password-protected (passwords are stored hashed, not in clear text).
