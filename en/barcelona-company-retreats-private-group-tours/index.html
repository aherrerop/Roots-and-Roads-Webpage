What we organize

Company retreats & offsites

A local-guided half-day that gets a visiting team into the real city — Sagrada Família, Gaudí’s Modernisme and the Gothic Quarter — as a shared experience, not a bus window. Slots into a retreat agenda around meetings and dinners.

Large groups, kept personal

Big group? We split it across several local guides so each sub-group stays small enough to ask questions — the same personal feel as our public tour, scaled by adding guides rather than a microphone.

International teams

Run parallel language streams — English, German, Spanish, Italian, French — at the same time, so everyone gets the tour in their own language.

Conferences & incentive trips

A memorable, low-logistics Barcelona experience for delegates: a fixed, easy meeting point and a walking route through the city’s highlights, tailored to your timing.

University, school & study groups

Context-rich, guide-led walks for student groups — real history and culture from local licensed guides, at a pace and depth you set.

Family reunions & celebrations

A private group walk for a reunion, a birthday or a special occasion — your route, your timing, your language.

FAQ

Can you handle a large group or a company retreat?

Yes — private, custom experiences for groups of any size. Large groups are split across several local guides so it stays personal, and can run in parallel language streams. Contact us to plan.

Is this the same as the free public tour?

No. The public tour is free to reserve, tip-based and kept small. A private group or company retreat is a separate, custom booking — your route, timing, languages and group size, quoted on request.

Which languages can you run for a group?

English, German, Spanish, Italian and French — and different language streams can run at the same time for an international group.
