# OTA Listing Optimization — Copy, Keywords & Phrasing

Ready-to-paste text for your GetYourGuide, Viator, GuruWalk and Airbnb listings, plus the keyword and phrasing rules behind them. The goal: rank on each platform's "best walking tours in Barcelona" category pages — which is exactly what AI assistants (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI) and travellers pull from.

> **Why this works:** live AI tests showed assistants rank tours on *keyword-relevant titles*, *review volume*, and a clear *differentiator*. Our honest edge is: **one tour connects BOTH the Gothic Quarter AND Gaudí/Sagrada Família** (most operators split those into two separate tours), in a **small group**, with a **local guide**, rated **4.92/5**. Lead with that everywhere.

## 7. Phrasing do's & don'ts (straight from the AI tests)

**DO**
- Lead with **"both the Gothic Quarter AND Gaudí/Sagrada Família in one tour"** — competitors split these; it's your clearest, honest edge.
- Say **"small group"**, **"local guide"**, **"ask questions"** — but always pair them with a concrete proof (the route, the rating), because everyone claims these.
- Name the sights the search engine indexes: **Sagrada Família, Gaudí, Passeig de Gràcia, Casa Batlló, Casa Milà, Gothic Quarter, Modernisme**.
- Use **"first day / first-time visitors / orientation"** — a huge share of tour searches are first-timers.
- Reference the **rating** where the platform allows ("rated 4.92/5 by guests").

**DON'T**
- Don't bury the differentiator below generic filler ("welcome to beautiful Barcelona…").
- Don't claim awards, "#1", or "best" you can't back up — OTAs can penalize it, and AIs discount it.
- Don't keyword-stuff so it reads unnaturally; conversion (bookings per view) is itself a top ranking factor, so the copy has to *sell*, not just list terms.
- Don't leave fields blank — listing **completeness** (every field filled, photos, itinerary stops) is a direct ranking signal.

## 8. The levers beyond copy (what actually moves OTA ranking)

Copy gets you keyword-relevance and click-through. These move ranking as much or more — worth pairing with the copy:

1. **Reviews — volume + recency.** The biggest single lever, and how you overtake "since 2010" operators over time. Ask every happy guest to review on that platform; recent reviews are weighted heavily.
2. **Conversion rate** (bookings ÷ views). A tight title + strong first line + good photos lift this, which lifts ranking further — a flywheel.
3. **Completeness & photos.** Fill every field (itinerary stops, inclusions, languages, meeting point, cancellation policy) and use bright, people-in-frame photos of the actual sights.
4. **Response & reliability.** Fast responses to questions, low cancellation rate, and honoring bookings all feed the platform's quality score.
5. **Category/attribute tags.** Tick every relevant category (Walking Tours, Historical Tours, Private/Small Group, City Tours, Cultural Tours) so you appear in more filtered "best of" lists.

Refresh the review count when it changes.*
